WHY SUDOGREP

Engineered for speed, built for privacy.

We follow strict principles when building apps, services, and online utilities.

100% Client-Side

Your data stays yours. All conversions, cropping, and PDF compiling happen on-device in your web browser. No remote uploads.

Zero Tracking

We do not load third-party analytics libraries, cookies, or advertisement pixels. The tools operate with absolute local privacy.

Vercel-Fast Performance

By compiling into pure static pages with zero bloated JS frameworks, our pages load instantly and score excellent Core Web Vitals.

Technical Design

Every product is minimal, technical, and built using native browser and operating system capabilities. No layout shifts.
