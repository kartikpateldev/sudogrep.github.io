How to Convert PNG to PDF

Converting a PNG image to a PDF document is useful for sharing screenshots, design previews, and charts as universally-readable files. This guide covers the conversion process, transparency handling, and page size selection.

PNG is a lossless raster image format commonly used for screenshots, UI designs, logos, and web graphics. PDF (Portable Document Format) is a universal document format that preserves layout, is widely printable, and can embed images in a structured container. Converting PNG to PDF is useful when you need to share an image as a printable document, submit a design for review, or bundle multiple screenshots into a single file.

How PNG to PDF Conversion Differs from JPG to PDF

The key difference between converting a PNG versus a JPG to PDF is transparency. PNG supports alpha channel transparency — areas of the image that are fully or partially transparent. When a transparent PNG is placed on a PDF page, the white page background shows through the transparent regions. This is typically the desired behavior for logos and icons. JPEG does not support transparency — all areas have a solid colour background.

Transparency Handling in PDF Context

When a PNG with a transparent background is placed on an A4 or Letter PDF page, the white page background becomes visible through the transparent areas. This is correct and expected behaviour. If you need the background to be a specific colour (not white), consider editing the PNG to fill the background with your desired colour before converting.

Page Size and Orientation Advice

Choose the page size based on where the PDF will be used:

  • A4: Standard for India, Europe, and most international contexts. 210mm × 297mm.
  • Letter: Standard for North America. 8.5in × 11in (216mm × 279mm).
  • Portrait: Use when your PNG is taller than it is wide (screenshots, portraits, infographics).
  • Landscape: Use when your PNG is wider than it is tall (charts, wide tables, panoramic images).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PNG transparency transfer to PDF?
PDF natively supports transparency, so transparent PNG regions will appear transparent on a white PDF background. The white page background will show through the transparent areas of your PNG.
Can I convert multiple PNG files to a single PDF?
Yes. SudoGrep's Image to PDF tool supports uploading multiple images simultaneously. Each image becomes a separate page in the compiled PDF document.
What page size should I use for PNG to PDF conversion?
For most document submissions, A4 (210mm × 297mm) is the standard page size. For US-style documents, use Letter (8.5in × 11in). Choose landscape orientation if your PNG is wider than it is tall.
