## 5. CTR Opportunities

The following pages have significant impressions and rank in competitive positions, but click-through rates are below average. **Action needed: Improve metadata and snippets.**

#### `/tools/image-compressor/`
- **Current Title**: `Image Compressor – Compress Images to KB Online`

- **Metrics**: 4,250 impressions | 118 clicks | 2.78% CTR | Position 5.2

1. **Request Indexation Re-crawl**: Submit `/guides/how-to-convert-multiple-images-to-pdf/` directly in GSC URL Inspection since it is marked 'Discovered - currently not indexed'.
