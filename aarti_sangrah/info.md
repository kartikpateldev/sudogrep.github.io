## Full description:

Aarti Sangrah is a comprehensive Hindu devotional app designed to bring over 300 sacred prayers, Aartis, Chalisas, Mantras, and Stotrams directly to your smartphone. Whether performing morning puja, observing festival fasts, or seeking spiritual peace, Aarti Sangrah helps you organize and read sacred scriptures effortlessly in a clean, distraction-free environment.

Organize your devotional routine with custom Pooja Playlists. Create personalized sequences for Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Diwali, or daily morning prayers. Arrange your Aartis in exact worship order, reorder them with drag-and-drop ease, and share your playlists with family and friends via direct web and app links.

Key Features of Aarti Sangrah:

• 300+ Sacred Devotional Texts: Access a vast collection of Aartis, Ashtakams, Mantras, Chalisas, Stotrams, Bhajans, Kathas, and Shlokas.

• Custom Pooja Playlists: Create, edit, and reorder prayer lists for festivals and daily worship routines. Sequence your prayers step-by-step.

• Multi-Language Support: Read devotional text seamlessly in 13 Indian languages, including English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Assamese, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.

• Categorized by Deity & Festival: Easily browse prayers dedicated to Lord Ganesha, Lord Krishna, Lord Shiva, Goddess Durga, Lord Rama, Hanuman Ji, Goddess Laxmi, and Surya Dev. Filter by major occasions such as Diwali, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Janmashtami, and Maha Shivratri.

• Customizable Reading Experience: Adjust font sizes (14pt to 32pt) for comfortable reading during rituals. Navigate smoothly between prayers with simple swipes.

• Smart Search & Instant Filters: Quickly search by prayer title, deity name, festival, or language with real-time suggestions and search history.

• Audio Aarti Support: Listen to selected audio Aartis directly within the app for a complete prayer experience.

• Offline Reading & Sync: Access cached prayers offline during worship. Sign in with Google to save and sync your favorite Aartis across devices.

Aarti Sangrah eliminates the need to carry physical prayer books or browse multiple websites during puja. Designed with clear typography, warm devotional aesthetics, and intuitive controls, Aarti Sangrah makes everyday worship simple, meaningful, and accessible.
