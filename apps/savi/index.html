Savi: Period & Cycle Tracker

Period & cycle tracker with ovulation, fertile window, symptoms and TTC tracking

GET IT ON Google Play

About the App

Savi is a simple period and cycle tracker designed to help you understand your menstrual cycle, track how you feel, and follow your fertile window when trying to conceive. Whether you want to keep track of your periods, understand your cycle patterns, or monitor fertility signs, Savi keeps the important information in one simple place. 🌸 TRACK YOUR PERIOD & CYCLE • Log your period and flow • See your current cycle day and cycle phase • View your cycle history and patterns • Get estimated upcoming period dates based on your cycle history • Understand your cycle without complicated charts 🌿 UNDERSTAND YOUR DAILY HEALTH Track the things that matter to you: • Symptoms • Mood • Energy • Sleep • Flow • Notes Keep your daily tracking simple and only record what you want to remember. 💗 TRYING TO CONCEIVE Savi can help you understand your estimated fertile window and ovulation timing based on your cycle history. You can also optionally track fertility signs such as: • LH test results • Basal body temperature (BBT) • Cervical mucus • Intercourse Savi uses your available cycle information to provide estimated fertile-window and ovulation information. These estimates can vary between cycles and should not be considered a guarantee of ovulation, fertility, conception, or pregnancy. 📅 SEE YOUR CYCLE CLEARLY Use the cycle calendar and timeline to see your periods, estimated fertile window, estimated ovulation and other important cycle information in one place. 🔒 YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL Savi is designed with privacy in mind. • Sign in with Google or use Guest access • Your cycle and health information is associated with your account when you choose an account-based experience • Delete your account and associated data from within the app • Your data is transmitted securely ✨ SIMPLE BY DESIGN Savi focuses on the information you actually need instead of overwhelming you with unnecessary features. Track your cycle. Understand your patterns. Know your estimated fertile days. Download Savi: Period & Cycle Tracker and make cycle tracking simpler. IMPORTANT HEALTH INFORMATION Savi is intended for tracking and informational purposes only. Cycle, fertile-window and ovulation information is estimated from the information you provide and may not be accurate for every person or every cycle. Savi does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care. If you have concerns about your menstrual health, fertility, pregnancy, symptoms, or other health conditions, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Savi predict ovulation and fertile windows?

Savi analyzes your historical cycle lengths and user-entered symptoms to calculate estimated fertile windows and ovulation timing.

Is my personal health and cycle data private?

Yes. Savi is designed with a strict privacy-first model. You can use Guest mode without an account, and your personal cycle logs remain completely under your control.

Can I use Savi when trying to conceive (TTC)?

Yes, Savi includes dedicated fertility tracking support including LH test results, basal body temperature (BBT), and cervical mucus monitoring.
