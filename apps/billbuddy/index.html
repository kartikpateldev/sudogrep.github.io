About the App

BillBuddy is a comprehensive personal finance organizer that tracks recurring payments, credit card bills, rent, loans, and subscriptions. Featuring automated reminders, calendar sync, spending category graphs, and local backups, it helps you manage your expenses seamlessly.

Key Features

  • ✓ Unified tracking of EMIs, bills, and subscriptions
  • ✓ Smart upcoming reminders and notifications
  • ✓ Visual calendar interface for due dates
  • ✓ Borrow/Lend records tracker
  • ✓ SMS payment parsing engine
  • ✓ CSV and PDF data export

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data stored in the cloud?

No. SudoGrep's BillBuddy app stores all your expense logs, bills, and notifications in local storage. We have no cloud database.

Can I export my bill statements?

Yes. You can export your data as CSV spreadsheet or compile a clean PDF statement on-device.
