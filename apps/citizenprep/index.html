About the App

Prepare smarter for your U.S. Citizenship Test with CitizenPrep! CitizenPrep helps you practice, learn, and build confidence with structured civics and history practice questions. Whether you're starting your preparation or doing a final review before your interview, CitizenPrep gives you a simple way to practice questions, track your progress, and identify areas where you need more focus. -------------------------------------------------- ⚠️ IMPORTANT GOVERNMENT DISCLAIMER & OFFICIAL SOURCES -------------------------------------------------- DISCLAIMER: CitizenPrep is an independent educational prep app and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or in any way officially connected to the United States Government, the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), or any other government agency. OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT SOURCES: All civics test questions and educational study materials provided in this app are based on publicly available official government resources from the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). You can access official and up-to-date government test resources directly from the official U.S. government website: • Official USCIS Civics Test Study Materials: https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test • Official USCIS Citizenship Resource Center: https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship -------------------------------------------------- KEY FEATURES -------------------------------------------------- 📚 Practice Civics & History Test Questions Test your knowledge with practice questions covering essential topics: • Government structure and institutions • Rights and responsibilities • American history and historical figures • Integrated civics, geography, and national symbols 🎯 Practice at Your Own Pace • Practice questions individually by topic • Take full-length timed mock practice tests • Review your answers and explanations • Learn from previous mistakes 📊 Track Your Learning Progress • Instant score calculation and pass/fail feedback • History tracking across all practice sessions • Question-level performance analytics 🔄 Offline Access & Guest Mode • Offline access allows you to practice anytime • Start practicing immediately in Guest mode with no account setup required

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CitizenPrep affiliated with the US Government?

No, CitizenPrep is an independent educational prep app. All civics questions and study materials are based on publicly available official test resources from the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).

Can I practice for the citizenship test offline?

Yes! All civics test questions, categories, and practice tests are stored locally on your device for convenient offline study.

Does CitizenPrep require an account?

No account setup is required. You can start practicing immediately in Guest mode with zero setup.
