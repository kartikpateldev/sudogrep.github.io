# Phase 7 — Search Performance Optimization

Only start this phase **after production has enough Google data**.

### Objective

Move from theoretical keyword targeting to optimization based on actual search behavior.

Collect:

* impressions
* clicks
* CTR
* average position
* queries
* pages receiving impressions

---

## Identify Three Categories

### A. High impressions / low CTR

Example:

```text
Impressions: 10,000
Clicks: 100
CTR: 1%
```

These pages may need:

* better title
* better meta description
* stronger search-intent alignment
* better SERP positioning

---

### B. High impressions / position 8–20

These are particularly valuable.

Prioritize them for:

* content improvements
* stronger internal links
* better headings
* supporting guides
* clearer answers
* improved topical coverage

---

### C. Queries you did not intentionally target

These are opportunities for future content.

For example:

```text
Query discovered
        ↓
Existing page relevant?
        ↓
YES → improve existing page
        ↓
NO
        ↓
Create new page only if intent is distinct
```

This becomes the basis for future content expansion.
