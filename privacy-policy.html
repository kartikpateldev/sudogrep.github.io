Privacy Policy

At SudoGrep, we prioritize user privacy. This Privacy Policy details our operational data principles across our website, products, and online utilities.

This policy outlines how SudoGrep handles data when you visit sudogrep.in or interact with our software systems.

1. Local Processing Commitment (On-Device)

All free web utilities listed under SudoGrep (including Image Compressor, Image Resizer, and Image to PDF compilers) are engineered to process data 100% locally.

  • No photo, document, signature, or PDF bytes are uploaded to remote servers.
  • All rendering and scaling transformations occur within your local browser window.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We only collect standard contact details when you choose to communicate with us:

  • Contact Submissions: If you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, email, selected services, and query contents to respond.
  • Communication Logs: Email correspondence sent directly to support queries is retained to coordinate development.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not use tracking cookies, retargeting advertisement pixels, or intrusive tracking networks on our website.

4. Data Security

We deploy strict physical and electronic access controls to safeguard contact database entries. We do not sell, rent, or trade contact metrics with third-party advertising companies.

5. Developer Page Listing

For information regarding mobile applications published on Google Play, check individual privacy links on the Google Play Console under SudoGrep's official listing.

Support Queries: If you have any inquiries regarding data deletion or local processing, contact our support team at support@sudogrep.in.
