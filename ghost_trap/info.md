🎯 HOW TO PLAY

Navigate Champ across the grid, carving paths to claim territory while avoiding bouncing ghosts. Claim 80%+ of each level to reveal breathtaking images of famous landmarks from around the world.

Each completed level brings you closer to mastering entire countries – from the Taj Mahal in India to Mount Fuji in Japan.

⚡ CORE GAMEPLAY

🗺️ Territory Claiming
Draw paths from safe zones into unclaimed territory. Connect back to safety to claim the enclosed area. But watch out – if a ghost touches your path before you finish, you lose a life!

💪 Strategic Power-Ups
• 🚀 Speeder – Move twice as fast for 30 seconds
• 🥶 Frost – Slow down ghosts by 50% for 30 seconds
• 🧊 Freeze – Stop all ghosts completely for 60 seconds

📈 Progressive Challenge
30 levels per stage with increasing difficulty. Early levels ease you in with 3 slow ghosts, while later levels challenge you with 8 fast-moving enemies.

🌍 World Exploration
Journey across countries and continents. Each stage represents a different nation with 30 unique landmarks to discover. Complete all levels to unlock the next country and earn bonus rewards.
