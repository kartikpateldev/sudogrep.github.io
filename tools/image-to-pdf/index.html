Convert Multiple Image Formats to a Single PDF

SudoGrep's free online image-to-pdf converter allows you to merge and compile multiple images of different formats (JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP) into a single, organized PDF document. All conversions and document packing are executed locally within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas rendering. Use Image to PDF when you need to combine different image formats or multiple images into a PDF. For a JPG/JPEG-specific workflow, use our JPG to PDF converter.

Page Sizes, Orientation, and Aspect Ratios

When generating your PDF document, you can configure several formatting parameters to match your layout requirements:

  • Fit to Image Size: Each page matches the exact pixel dimensions of the uploaded image. This preserves the original photo aspect ratio without adding borders or margins.
  • A4 (Standard Document): Places each image onto an A4 page layout (595 × 842 points) with padding, scaling images down as needed.
  • US Letter: Fits images into US Letter document sizes (612 × 792 points) centered with standard paddings.
  • Orientation: Choose between Portrait or Landscape layout guides to orient pages correctly.
  • Drag Reordering: Drag and drop the selected image cards in the list to rearrange the page sequence before compiler export.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert multiple images into a single PDF?
Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP images into the upload zone, drag the list cards to arrange the page sequence, select your target page size (A4, Letter, or Fit to Image), and click Generate PDF.
What is the difference between Image to PDF and JPG to PDF?
Use Image to PDF when you need to combine different image formats or multiple images into a PDF. For a JPG/JPEG-specific workflow, use our JPG to PDF converter.
Which page sizes and layouts are supported?
We support Fit to Image Size (page size matches image pixels), A4 standard printing pages, and US Letter. You can also specify Portrait or Landscape orientations.
Is my document compiled on a server?
No. All binary streams, canvas rendering, and PDF document compilations are done locally inside your browser sandbox. SudoGrep never uploads your images.
